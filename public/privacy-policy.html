Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 14, 2026 | Effective Date: October 14, 2026

Hope3 Foundation ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile application Hope3 Apps (the "App"). We are dedicated to providing educational support, scholarships, mentorship, and community coordination for students and donors. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal information collected through the App.

Core Principle: We do not sell your personal data. We do not engage in third-party ad tracking. Any data collected is strictly used to facilitate educational sponsorship, student progress tracking, and attendance verification.

1. Information We Collect

To provide the services in Hope3 Apps, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account & Identity Information: Name, email address, phone number, academic roll/registration numbers, and student ID provided during authentication (via Google Sign-In or administrative registration).
  • Location Data:
    • Precise Location: Accessed while using the App (and periodically in the background when geofenced session tracking is active) exclusively to verify class, event, or workshop attendance within approved Hope3 learning zones.
    • Location coordinates are compared against pre-configured geofence boundaries and are not shared with advertisers or third parties.
  • User Content & Media (Camera and Photos):
    • Photos and document scans taken with your camera or chosen from your photo gallery.
    • This media is used exclusively by students to upload marksheets, grade reports, certificates, and profile documentation for academic review.
  • Device & Diagnostic Data: Device model, operating system version, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens required to deliver push notifications regarding exam schedules, announcements, and mentor updates.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To authenticate and manage student, donor, and administrator accounts.
  • To verify student presence and attendance during scheduled academic sessions using geofencing.
  • To review, verify, and track semester marksheets and learning roadmaps.
  • To deliver real-time administrative notices, alerts, and mentorship feedback.
  • To ensure app stability, detect performance issues, and protect account security.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with trusted service providers to run secure infrastructure. These services process data in compliance with strict privacy standards:

  • Google Firebase / Google Identity: Authentication, cloud database storage, and push notification delivery (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Apple Sign-In: Secure authentication for iOS users.
  • OpenStreetMap / Maps Toolkit: Map rendering and geofence distance calculation.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information is only shared under the following limited conditions:

  • Foundation Staff & Mentors: Authorized foundation coordinators review student progress, marksheets, and attendance.
  • Sponsoring Donors: Aggregated or designated academic progress reports are shared with authorized donors who sponsor specific students.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, regulation, or legal process to protect the rights and safety of the foundation or users.

5. Data Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard technical measures (including HTTPS/TLS encryption for data in transit and secure database access controls) to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We retain student data for the duration of their active educational program and as necessary for historical grant and foundation records.

6. Account & Data Deletion (User Rights)

In accordance with App Store guidelines, you have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

To request the deletion of your account, profile, marksheets, or location history:

  • Email us at contact@hope3.org with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and your registered email address.
  • Your account and associated personal identifiers will be permanently removed within 30 days of request verification.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is designed for students enrolled in secondary and higher education programs. For students under 18 years of age, access and enrollment are coordinated with school administrators, parents, or legal guardians through Hope3 Foundation's established scholarship programs.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". Continued use of the App following any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
